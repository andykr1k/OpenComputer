# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenComputer contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and help contributors learn.
- Respect privacy, consent, and responsible-use boundaries.
- Disclose conflicts of interest relevant to a discussion or decision.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or project infrastructure.
- Encouraging abusive, deceptive, privacy-invasive, or unlawful computer automation.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior conflicts with this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect the privacy of people reporting concerns.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/andykr1k). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
